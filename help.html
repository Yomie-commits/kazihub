Freelance services

The buyer places an order and describes what they need. The seller accepts, does the work and marks it delivered. The buyer then approves it (or asks for a revision) and leaves a review.

For now, agree on payment (for example M-Pesa) with your client in KaziHub messages once the order is accepted. Keep all agreements in writing on KaziHub so we can help if something goes wrong.
