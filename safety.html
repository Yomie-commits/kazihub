Stay safe on KaziHub

Simple rules that protect you from scams and bad deals.

Never pay to get a job

Real employers and licensed agencies do not charge workers application, “processing”, medical or visa fees before a written offer. If someone asks, stop and report them.

Check the agency

For overseas jobs, confirm the recruitment agency is licensed by Kenya's National Employment Authority (NEA) before sharing documents.

Keep it on KaziHub

Agree on work, price and timelines in KaziHub messages. Written records help us resolve disputes.

Protect your documents

Only share copies of your ID or passport after you have a written offer, and never hand over your original passport to an agent in Kenya.

Meet safely for local jobs

For juakali work, tell a friend where you are going, confirm the job details in messages first, and trust your instincts.

Report anything suspicious

Use the Report button on any listing, or contact us. Our team reviews every report.

Think you've been targeted by a scam?

Stop all payments, keep your messages, and tell us right away.
